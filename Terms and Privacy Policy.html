Privacy Policy

What we collect

  • Account info: name, email, phone number, profile photo.
  • Content you create: challenges, check-in photos and notes, ratings, cheers.
  • Social info: your friends/contacts list and who you've invited.

How we use it

  • To operate your account and show your data back to you across devices.
  • To show your public or friends-shared content to the appropriate people.
  • To send invite links and notifications you trigger (e.g. sharing a challenge).

What we don't do

We don't sell your personal information. We don't share your private (solo or friends-only) challenge data with anyone outside the people you've explicitly invited.

Your choices

  • You can set any challenge to solo, friends-only, or public.
  • You can delete your account and data at any time from your profile.
  • You can stop sharing your location/contacts at any time — Soft Sapling only uses what you choose to share.
